Executive Summary

The Strategic Case for Arua SEZ

Arua Special Economic Zone is Uganda's premier cross-border industrial platform, located in Arua City within 100km of DRC and 160km of South Sudan. The zone offers a complete industrial ecosystem backed by Uganda's 19-point statutory incentive framework.

Investor Incentives

19-Point Statutory Incentive Framework

  • 10-year corporate income tax holiday
  • 0% import duty on plant & machinery
  • VAT exemption on zone inputs
  • Full foreign exchange repatriation
  • 100% capital repatriation on exit
  • Withholding tax exemptions on dividends
  • No minimum capital requirements
  • One-stop licensing clearance (21 days)
  • Work permit fast-track for foreign staff
  • 49-year land-use leases available
